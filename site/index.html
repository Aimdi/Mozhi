Private, beautiful translation — Mozhi engines and the AI of your choice

Features

Everything a translator should do. Nothing it shouldn’t.

Fast everyday translation with the depth of an AI language tutor, on your phone, your desktop or in the browser.

  • Every Mozhi engine

    Google, DeepL, Yandex, Reverso, LibreTranslate, MyMemory and DuckDuckGo through any Mozhi instance, with automatic failover across your list. Or bring your own DeepL API key or LibreTranslate server.

  • The AI of your choice

    OpenAI-compatible APIs, Anthropic, Gemini and Ollama. Tune every translation the way Kagi Translate does:

    • Natural or literal
    • Formality
    • Reading level
    • Context
    • Glossary
    • Custom styles

  • Refine, don’t start over

    Tap More formal, Shorter or Simpler to edit the translation you have. Changes stack, opposites replace each other, and a second tap undoes one.

  • Understand, not just translate

    Alternatives for the whole text or a selected phrase, word insights and Explain this translation. Tap a phrase to see its match in both texts, and read non-Latin scripts in romanization.

  • A dictionary built in

    Definitions, pronunciation, examples and related words in three levels of detail, with Did you mean…? for typos.

  • Smart about languages

    Says when a short word could be in several languages, switches the target when your text is already in it (with an undo), and keeps your recent language pairs one tap away.

  • Compare engines

    Run several engines side by side and keep the result that reads best.

  • Fast or Best

    Pick a quick everyday engine and your strongest model once, then switch between them with one tap, just like Kagi’s quality modes.

  • Proofread

    Fix spelling and grammar with clear explanations, in the style, correction level, reading level and regional variety you choose, with writing statistics before and after.

  • Translate images

    Snap a menu, a sign or a screenshot, or pick a photo, and read it in your language.

  • Voice in, voice out

    Dictate instead of typing, and listen to any translation read aloud.

  • Documents and web pages

    Translate whole documents, or open a web page and read it in your language. Long texts are split at paragraph boundaries and keep their context.

  • Works offline

    Download on-device language packs (Google ML Kit) on Android and iOS and translate with no connection at all.

  • History and favourites

    Search everything you have translated, star the keepers, export or import it all as JSON, or let older entries delete themselves after 1, 7 or 30 days.

  • Know what AI costs

    Requests, characters and estimated tokens per provider each month, with an optional budget that warns you before a big bill.

  • Translate from any app

    On Android, select text anywhere and tap Translate with Mozhi, even replacing it in place, or share text straight to the app.

  • Links and shortcuts

    Kagi Translate-style links and mozhi:// links open pre-filled but never start a paid request on their own. Keyboard shortcuts include Alt+1…9 for pinned languages.

  • Feels at home everywhere

    Material You colours, dark and pure-black themes, and adaptive layouts for phones, tablets and desktops.

How it works

Three ways to translate. You decide where your words go.

Use one, or all three side by side. The app has no servers of its own: it talks straight to the backend you choose.

  • Mozhi instance

    Trusted engines, without the tracking

    You Mozhi Engines

    Mozhi is an open-source front-end that forwards your text to engines like Google, DeepL or Yandex, so they see the instance, not you. Use a built-in public instance or host your own.

    Who sees your text: the instance, and the engine it forwards to.

  • AI provider

    Nuanced, context-aware translation

    You Your AI provider

    Add your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or any OpenAI-compatible URL. No middleman, no extra account, and you choose the model.

    Who sees your text: only the provider you configured.

  • Local model

    Nothing leaves your network

    You Your computer

    Run an open model with Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp and connect over your Wi-Fi, or download offline language packs and translate on the phone itself.

    Who sees your text: nobody but you.

Host your own Mozhi in a minute

Run this on any machine with Docker, then add http://<server-ip>:3000 under Settings › Mozhi instances and move it to the top.

Terminal 
docker run -d --name mozhi \
  --restart unless-stopped \
  -p 3000:3000 \
  codeberg.org/aryak/mozhi:latest

Privacy

Your words are nobody’s business

The app has no servers, no accounts and no business model built on what you translate.

  • No account

    Open the app and translate. No sign-up, no email, no login.

  • No tracking

    No telemetry, no analytics, no ads. The app never phones home.

  • History stays with you

    History, favourites and settings live on your device, and leave it only when you export them.

  • Keys in secure storage

    API keys are kept in the platform’s secure storage and sent only to the provider they belong to.

  • You pick the destination

    Text goes only to the instance or provider you chose. Self-host both and nothing leaves your network.

  • Open source

    MIT licensed. Read every line, build it yourself or fork it.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this the official Mozhi app?

No. Mozhi is a privacy-friendly translation front-end created by aryak and contributors. This app is an independent, unofficial client that talks to Mozhi instances through their public API, and adds AI translation on top.

Do I need to run my own server?

No. A list of public Mozhi instances is built in, and the app fails over to the next one if an instance is down. If you prefer, self-host Mozhi with the one-line Docker command above and move your instance to the top of the list.

Which AI providers can I use?

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, Together, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and local runtimes: Ollama, LM Studio and the llama.cpp server. Use Test connection in the provider editor to try a tiny translation before saving. The DeepL API and your own LibreTranslate server can be added in the same place.

What do Fast and Best do?

They work like Kagi Translate’s quality modes. In Settings › Quality modes, pick a quick, cheap engine (say Google through Mozhi) as Fast and your strongest AI model as Best. A Fast / Best toggle next to the engine button then switches between them in one tap, and a link with text can ask for one for that text with quality=fast or quality=best.

Can a link or shared text run up my AI bill?

Not on their own. Links and text shared to the app fill in the translator, but with an AI model you press Translate yourself (unless you turn on instant translation for AI); Mozhi engines translate them straight away. Settings › Usage & costs shows how much each provider was used this month, with an optional budget warning.

How do I use a model running on my computer from my phone?

On a phone, localhost is the phone itself, so use your computer’s network address, for example http://192.168.1.20:11434. Then let the server listen on the network: start Ollama with OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0, enable Serve on Local Network in LM Studio, or run llama-server --host 0.0.0.0. Instruction-tuned models of about 7B parameters or more give good results.

Does it work offline?

Yes. On Android and iOS you can download on-device language packs and translate without any connection. A local model on your home network also works without the internet.

Why does the web app need CORS for my own servers?

Browsers only let a web page call servers that allow it with CORS headers. For a self-hosted Mozhi instance, add them in your reverse proxy; for Ollama, set OLLAMA_ORIGINS; in LM Studio, enable CORS. The Android and desktop apps do not need this.

Which platforms are supported?

Android APKs are attached to every release: pick arm64-v8a for almost every modern phone, armeabi-v7a for older 32-bit devices, or universal if unsure. The web app runs in any modern browser, and iOS, macOS and Windows can be built from source with Flutter.

How do I check that a download is genuine?

Each release includes a SHA256SUMS.txt file. Compare it with the output of sha256sum mozhi-*.apk before installing.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and open source under the MIT License, and Mozhi instances are free to use. Commercial AI providers bill you for their API according to their own pricing; local models cost nothing.
