Mozhi Translate is a private translator with no account, no ads and no tracking. Translate on your phone, through Mozhi servers (an open-source front-end for Google, DeepL and more) or with the AI model you choose, in the cloud or on your own computer.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Offline first: translated on the phone when the language pair is installed; otherwise Mozhi asks before your text goes online. Or choose Offline only or Online
• Your text goes only to services you chose, and each translation names who received it
• E-mail addresses, phone numbers, IBANs, card numbers and IP addresses stay on the phone when text goes online
• Encrypted history, kept out of Android's cloud backup; incognito, an app lock and a list of every network request
• No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no Google code

OFFLINE ON THE PHONE
• About 55 languages with Mozilla's Bergamot engine and the Firefox Translations models
• Speech recognition with Whisper, text recognition with Tesseract and on-device AI models
• Downloaded only when you tap them and checked before use, or imported from files. Their publishers (Mozilla on Google Cloud, Hugging Face, GitHub) see your IP address unless you use a mirror

ONLINE WHEN YOU WANT
• Public Mozhi servers built in, or your own, with automatic failover. Public servers are run by other people: they and their engine can read what you send them
• OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or any OpenAI-compatible server with your own key; local models with Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp; the DeepL API and LibreTranslate
• Compare engines side by side; a Fast and a Best engine one tap apart

AI EXTRAS
• Tone, reading level (CEFR A1 to C2), gender, context, glossaries and presets; custom targets such as "Pirate speak"
• Refine (More formal, Shorter, Simpler and more), alternatives, insights and explanations
• Check meaning: a back-translation that flags differences in meaning
• Proofreading and a dictionary

CAMERA, DOCUMENTS AND VOICE
• Live camera and photos: the text is read on the phone and the translation drawn over it
• Documents keep their format and layout: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, EPUB, subtitles and localisation files
• Conversation face to face, or shared with guests' phones on the same Wi-Fi
• Transcribe talks and caption other apps' sound (Android 10 and later), recognised on the phone

EVERYDAY USE
• Instant translation as you type, language detection, tap a word to look it up
• "Translate with Mozhi" and "Translate offline" in the selection menu of any app; translate the screen, share to Mozhi, Quick Settings tiles and a widget
• Favorites, phrasebooks and flashcard practice
• Encrypted backups, sync through your own WebDAV server, phone-to-phone transfer
• Material You, dark themes, tablet layouts and screen-reader labels

Mozhi Translate is free and open-source software (MIT), an independent and unofficial client: the Mozhi front-end (codeberg.org/aryak/mozhi) is a separate project, and the app is not affiliated with Google, DeepL or any AI provider.
