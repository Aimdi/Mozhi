Mozhi Translate is a fast, private translator. It translates through Mozhi instances (an open-source front-end for Google, DeepL, Reverso, Yandex, LibreTranslate, DuckDuckGo and MyMemory) and through the AI models you choose, in the cloud or on your own computer, with the extras you know from Kagi Translate.

<b>Translation engines</b>
• Every engine your Mozhi instance offers, with public instances built in and automatic failover between them
• Your own self-hosted Mozhi instance, also over plain http on your home network
• AI providers: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, Together, or any OpenAI-compatible server
• Local models with Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp or vLLM, marked with a "Local" badge
• Compare mode: all engines side by side, keep the best result
• Offline translation with on-device language packs

<b>AI extras</b>
• Natural or literal style, formality, reading level (CEFR A1 to C2), speaker and addressee gender, context and glossary
• Custom targets such as "Pirate speak", "Plain English" or a dialect
• Alternatives, word insights with other ways to say it, one-tap refinements (more formal, shorter, simpler, ...), "Explain this translation" and a dictionary for words and short phrases
• Proofreading with corrections and explanations
• AI extras also work while a Mozhi engine translates

<b>Everyday use</b>
• Translate as you type, automatic language detection, swap, transliteration, text-to-speech
• "Translate with Mozhi" in the text selection menu of any app, with the option to replace the selected text, and a share target
• Translate photos, voice and documents
• Searchable history and favourites, with JSON export and import
• Material You colours, dark and pure-black themes, layouts for phones, tablets and foldables

<b>Privacy</b>
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry
• History and settings stay on your device; API keys are kept in secure storage
• Your text is only sent to the Mozhi instance or AI provider you select, or nowhere at all with local and offline models
• Offline translation uses Google's ML Kit libraries, which download language packs from Google and report API usage metrics to Google; your text stays on the device

Mozhi Translate is free and open-source software (MIT). It is an independent, unofficial client: the Mozhi translation front-end (codeberg.org/aryak/mozhi) is a separate project by its own authors, and the app is not affiliated with Google, DeepL, Kagi or any AI provider.
